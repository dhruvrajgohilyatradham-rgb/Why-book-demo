Accommodation Information

Sri Guru Sadan is located 1.2 km from the Sri Mallikarjuna Swamy Temple. The accommodation offers two-bed AC rooms, two-bed non-AC rooms, three-bed AC mini suite rooms, and four-bed AC suite rooms. The stay provides facilities like CCTV, lifts, hot water, clean drinking water and a Garden for your comfortable stay.

Meals and parking facilities are also available here. The check-in and check-out times of Sri Gurusadan are at 12:00 PM and 11:00 AM. Stay near the temple in the top dharamshalas in Srisailam. Easily Book Rooms in Srisailam for a comfortable stay near the temple and enjoy a peaceful visit.

Srisailam is home to two famous temples - Mallikarjuna Swamy Jyotirling and Bhramarambha temples. It is rare for a Jyotirling temple and a Shakti Peeth to be located side by side. Srisailam is situated amid natural beauty and is surrounded by hills, forests, lakes and wildlife sanctuaries. Though Srisailam is an ancient pilgrimage, it was further developed during the Vijayanagara reign.
